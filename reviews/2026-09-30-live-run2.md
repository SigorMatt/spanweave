## The two quadratics in `feed` — confirmed, and there are three

Both sites named in the L5 resume note are real and correctly located. The
note understates one of them and misses a third.

**(i) Wide shape, sibling-group temporal chain — CONFIRMED.**
`SpanAbsorber._place` (`incremental.py:442-458`) ends every arrival with
`self._restate_chain(group)`; `_restate_chain` (`:475-485`) rebuilds the
whole group:

```python
members = self._groups.get(group, set())
self._temporal_edges[group] = tuple(
    build.temporal_chain(self._nodes[position] for position in members))
self._tally.set_edges(("temporal", group), self._temporal_edges[group])
```

`build.temporal_chain` (`build.py:940-970`) does
`itertools.pairwise(sorted(siblings, key=tie_break))` and allocates a fresh
`Edge` per consecutive pair — m-1 new objects for a group of m, reusing
none of the m-2 that did not change. In the wide shape every child shares
parent `s0`, so there is one group and m grows to n: O(m log m) per
arrival. Profile at k=2000: `temporal_chain` called 2000 times,
`Edge.__init__` called **1,999,000** times = Σm.

**(ii) Echo shape, a call id's `data` edge set — CONFIRMED, and it is
cubic, not quadratic.** `_index_calls` (`incremental.py:359-362`) appends
to `self._receipts[call_id]`; `_restate_calls` (`:402-428`) then calls
`build.data_edges(call_id, self._receipts.get(call_id, ()), ...)` per
touched call id. `build.data_edges` (`build.py:865-892`) does
`sorted(set(receivers))` over the *entire* receipt list and re-emits an
`Edge` per (receiver, fulfiller) pair, because `basis` (`DATA_BASIS` vs
`DATA_LATER_BASIS`) depends on which receipt ranks first — a fact about the
whole set. The echo LLM span at turn *t* touches *t+1* call ids each
holding ~(t-j) receipts, so one arrival is O(t²) and the whole feed is
**cubic in turns**. Profile at 150 turns: `data_edges` called 11,475 times
(= Σ(t+1)), `Edge.__init__` 618,950 ≈ turns³/6.

**(iii) A third superlinear site the note missed — a late-arriving
parent.** `_absorb_at` (`incremental.py:327-333`) restates every record
waiting on the arriving span id; `_restate_parent` ends with `_regroup`
(`:388-389`), and `_regroup` (`:460-469`) calls `_restate_chain` **twice**.
If the root arrives after its n children, that single `feed` does 2n full
chain rebuilds — O(n² log n) inside one call. Measured on the wide shape
with the root fed last: n=500 → 934 ms, n=1000 → 4306 ms, n=2000 →
**15352 ms for one arrival**.

Ruled out as superlinear: `Builder.feed` / `_translate`
(`api.py:168-185,263-285`) O(1); `_restate_whole_input`
(`incremental.py:262-282`) guarded by `_whole_input_from`;
`_restate_every_record` (`:249-261`) O(n) but only on a whole-input fact
change; `Ledger.add` / `drop` O(1) (`edge_key` is `edge.identity`);
`Journal.record` O(1). Retention is not a factor — `retain(0)` reproduces
the same times.

### Measured growth

Wide shape, feed only — no `graph()` and no `delta()` in the timed region.
One run per k, CPython 3.14.6, HEAD `2cde61f`. `tests/live_cost.py` was
extended in a throwaway worktree and **not** committed.

```
      k    total s   ms/record  ms/rec vs k0  total vs k0
   1000       1.91       1.908         1.00x         1.0x
   2000       7.80       3.900         2.04x         4.1x
   4000      34.57       8.643         4.53x        18.1x
   8000     172.08      21.510        11.27x        90.2x
```

Per-record cost is **not flat** — it rises 2.04x, 2.22x, 2.49x across the
three doublings. Per-record feed is linear in k (slightly above, from
allocator/GC drift over a heap that grows with the graph), so total feed is
quadratic. Growth is shown, not inferred.

On the note's arithmetic: the measured k=1000→k=8000 total ratio is
**90.2x, not 116x**. It supports the shape of the claim — 8x the records
costs far more than 8x, and more than the 64x of a clean quadratic — but
116x is ~30% optimistic for this machine. See N2: the number belongs with
its provenance, or in the harness, not bare in `SPEC.md`.
